# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive community. All contributors and users agree to:
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Provide constructive feedback and accept feedback graciously.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Prioritise security and privacy of all stakeholders.

Incidents can be reported to the maintainers via the issue tracker or email listed in `SECURITY_POLICY.md`.
